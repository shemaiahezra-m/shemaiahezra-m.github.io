Volume 05 / About Me

Personal notes on design, development, and the way I think through digital work.

I started with visual communication before becoming curious about the interfaces and systems behind the work I was designing.

Story

Design became my entry point into technology.

I started exploring publication materials, social media graphics, and youth media projects. Over time, that creative foundation led me toward Information Technology and user-centered digital experiences.

Design Journey

Visual hierarchy taught me how to guide attention.

Editorial layouts, campaign materials, and branding systems shaped how I think about clarity, pacing, typography, and emotional tone.

Frontend Journey

I like turning visual ideas into working interfaces.

My current focus is UI/UX design, frontend development, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, and MySQL-backed systems.

Interests

Creative systems, useful interfaces, and quiet details.

I enjoy digital products, visual communication, student projects, community media, and interfaces that feel polished without becoming complicated.

Creative Philosophy

Good design should feel intentional before it asks to be noticed.

I care about work that is clean, accessible, functional, and visually engaging, with enough restraint for the content to breathe.
