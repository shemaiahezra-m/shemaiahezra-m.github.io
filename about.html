SHEMAIAH EZRA MAGPAYO · THIRD-YEAR BSIT

To make things clear, useful, and human.

I’m a third-year BSIT student at Polytechnic University of the Philippines, Sto. Tomas, creating thoughtful interfaces, useful frontend experiences, and multimedia work.

I design and build clean, user-centered digital experiences that are functional, accessible, and visually engaging. My journey into technology began through design: in 2018, I started exploring graphic design and creative media; by 2021, I was creating publication materials, social media graphics, and visual campaigns.

Over the years, I worked on creative campaigns, branding materials, event publicity, and digital content for various organizations. As I continued creating, I became curious about the systems and interfaces behind the visuals. In 2024, I began my BSIT journey. Today, I explore UI/UX design, frontend development, and user-centered digital experiences.

Video editing was my first love: I started with transitions and stayed for storytelling. The publication materials got serious; one layout at a time, they turned into products. Most of my hobbies became responsibilities. Fortunately, I still enjoy them.

I never expected that learning basic HTML in Grade 7, something I initially saw as just another school requirement, would eventually become part of the path I chose in college. Before discovering my passion for technology and design, I actually thought I would pursue a career in medicine. Looking back, that early exposure to web development became the first step toward the field I enjoy today.

Let’s build something meaningful. Whether it’s a design project, a website, or a creative collaboration, I’d love to connect.
